# DPIA Template: Customer-Facing AI Chatbot

A Data Protection Impact Assessment structure for AI chatbots: customer-facing systems using LLM APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google) to handle support, sales, or information queries.

## 5. Risk Assessment

**5.1 Inappropriate disclosures by the chatbot**
- Likelihood: medium (LLMs can hallucinate or echo training data)
- Severity: depends on what data is in scope
- Mitigation: System prompt restrictions, content filtering, escalation triggers

**5.2 Personal data leaking to LLM provider beyond what's needed**
- Likelihood: high without controls
- Mitigation: PII filtering on your server before API call, data minimisation in prompt construction

**5.3 Conversation logs becoming a high-value attack surface**
- Likelihood: low if encryption + access control applied
- Mitigation: encryption at rest, role-based access, retention limits

**5.4 Vendor lock-in or LLM provider data breach**
- Likelihood: low but consequential
- Mitigation: portable conversation data, vendor due diligence, breach notification clause in DPA

**5.5 User assumption that chatbot is human (Article 13 transparency)**
- The EU AI Act (and good practice) requires disclosure that this is an AI system
- Mitigation: Clear disclosure at the start of every conversation

**5.6 Data subject rights handling**
- DSAR for chatbot conversations needs to surface all interactions a user had
- Right to erasure needs to delete conversation history end-to-end (including LLM provider's logs if any)
- Mitigation: Clear DSAR/erasure process tied to user identifier, including process to confirm deletion at LLM provider where applicable
